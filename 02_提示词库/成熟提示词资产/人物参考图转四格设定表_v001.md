- 验证：用户提供方法；本轮补全与文本检查；媒体未测、身份一致性未测、具体平台适配未执行。

## 补全和修订依据

| 原文问题 | 本版处理 | 依据与边界 |
|---|---|---|
| `MAGE TASK` | 改为 `IMAGE TASK` | 明显缺首字母的语义修正 |
| `BODY: no pose change between , no , no height change` | `BODY: no pose change between BODY FRONT and BACK apart from the viewing direction, no body-proportion drift, no height change.` | **按逻辑补写，不是恢复原文**；约束同站姿、同体型、同身高，全身正背仍允许必要观察方向变化 |
| FACE 末尾停在 `no` | 补为 `no facial asymmetry changes` | **按身份一致性逻辑补写，不是恢复原文**；保留原有天然不对称，不做美颜对称化 |
| 硬光、无补光、背景与光色占位 | 中性灰背景、左前扩散大柔光、轻柔填充、中性光色 | 按用户已确认的柔光规范改编；不保留硬光版为默认 |

| 四格和分辨率未给画幅目标 | 9:16、2×2、2160×3840 目标 | 提示词是目标描述，实际像素以产物核验 |

## 可复制正文

先在当前入口上传符合前述条件的参考图，再复制整个代码块。只复制正文，不把资产 ID、验证状态或本节说明发送给图片模型。

```text
IMAGE TASK: Convert the uploaded photographs of one character into a single 4-panel character reference sheet. Show the same person in every panel, preserving the supplied identity and clothing.

CRITICAL PRESERVATION:
Use the uploaded images as the only design source. Preserve the visible facial structure, eye shape, nose, mouth shape, hairline, hairstyle, age, skin tone, natural skin texture, existing facial asymmetry, body proportions and weight. Preserve every visible garment detail, including cut, fabric, seams, wear, hardware and footwear. Preserve existing moles, glasses and facial hair when present in the source. Keep the character's established design consistent across all four views.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
One portrait image with a 9:16 aspect ratio and a 4K output target of 2160 by 3840 pixels. Arrange four equal panels in a 2x2 grid with thin gutters. Put a small label in each panel corner: FACE FRONT, FACE SIDE, BODY FRONT, BACK.

PANEL 1 — TOP LEFT — FACE FRONT:
A head-and-shoulders portrait facing the camera directly. The facial muscles are relaxed, the lips rest naturally together, and the eyes look forward with a neutral resting expression. Facial features, hairline and identifying details are clearly readable.

PANEL 2 — TOP RIGHT — FACE SIDE:
A head-and-shoulders profile at the same scale as PANEL 1. The camera observes the character's anatomical right side, showing the right ear and right facial outline. Preserve the same resting expression, facial proportions and hairstyle. Keep asymmetric details on their correct anatomical sides.

PANEL 3 — BOTTOM LEFT — BODY FRONT:
A full-body front view with the entire head and both shoes inside the panel. The character stands naturally upright with a comfortable, balanced stance and both arms relaxed at the sides. Preserve the source body's proportions and the source outfit. Facial detail is naturally smaller at this full-body scale while the facial structure remains consistent with PANELS 1 and 2.

PANEL 4 — BOTTOM RIGHT — BACK:
A full-body rear view at the same full-body scale and camera height as PANEL 3. Show the back of the hairstyle, garment structure and seams, trousers and shoe heels. Maintain the same natural standing posture, body proportions, arm position and foot placement relative to the body. The viewing direction changes to reveal the back.

STUDIO SETUP:
Use the same clean, seamless neutral-gray studio background in all four panels. Light the subject with a large diffused soft key light at 45 degrees camera-left and gentle soft fill. Use neutral-white illumination, even exposure, soft shadow transitions and natural contact shadows under the shoes. Keep the background plain and the surrounding space clear.

QUALITY:
A photographic character sheet for production reference. Preserve natural skin texture, realistic matte skin, accurate fabric weave, readable garment construction and crisp structural detail. Render cleanly with consistent color and exposure across the panels.

AVOID:
FACE: beauty-filter skin, glossy plastic complexion, added idol makeup, face slimming, enlarged eyes, facial asymmetry changes, expression changes, erased facial features, redesigned hair.
BODY: pose changes between BODY FRONT and BACK apart from the viewing direction, body-proportion drift, height changes, outfit changes, mirrored asymmetric details, extra limbs, missing hands, cropped heads or cropped shoes.
LIGHT AND FRAME: direct hard light, dramatic rim lighting, ambient color spill, visible image noise, background clutter, extra people, added handheld props, watermarks, extra text beyond the four panel labels.
```

## 使用后检查

逐格核对同一人、五官和发型、痣／眼镜等已选细节、人物自身左右、全身比例、原衣装、姿态、背景和柔光；用实际文件读取像素，不依据正文中的 4K 推断。标签字形若生成失败，可保留干净无字图并在制作软件标注，记录后期处理。

图像通过后，Clip 默认只锚定面部、五官与发型；明确指定的痣／眼镜等可加入身份范围。每条 Clip 的衣装由正文独立定义，不能把本转换词的“保留原衣装”误传成视频衣装继承规则。
